Privacy Policy

Seventh Sense Uploader · Last updated October 12, 2026

Who uses this app

Seventh Sense Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Seventh Sense YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses only the owner's own Google account:

  • YouTube: upload and edit the channel's own videos, thumbnails, captions and channel details; read the channel's own comments and analytics.
  • Google Docs: read and update the owner's own documents.
  • Basic profile (email address): to confirm which account was authorized.

What it does not do

It does not collect information about anyone else, does not sell or share data, does not use data for advertising, and does not transfer data to third parties.

Where data is kept

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on this website.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube access is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
